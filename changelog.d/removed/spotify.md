- Spotify is gone: MyMusic now plays YouTube Music and the music on your
  computer. Its sign-in, search, library, podcasts, Spotify Connect, the
  Spotify tracks setting and the `spotifyClientId` setting are removed.
  On the first start, MyMusic forgets the Spotify sign-in, and takes
  Spotify out of your liked and disliked songs, mixes, history, and the
  artists and shows you follow, after keeping a copy of the library as
  it was in `library.json.spotify`.
