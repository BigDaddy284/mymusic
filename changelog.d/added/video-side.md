- A Video side beside the music: clicking the mark at the top of the
  sidebar spins it over and switches between Music and Video. Video
  shows YouTube's own home feed, subscriptions, history, Watch later,
  liked videos and playlists with the YouTube Music sign-in, a search,
  Trending, and a Random button that searches for a random Wikipedia
  article. Videos play in YouTube's player, keep playing small while
  you browse, fill the window with Full App, and the player bar
  controls them with the same buttons as the music.
- The sidebar and the side panel can be dragged to any width.
