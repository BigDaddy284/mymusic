- A Video side beside the music: clicking the mark at the top of the
  sidebar spins it over and switches between Music and Video. Video's
  Home shows YouTube's own home feed, and beside it your subscriptions,
  history, playlists, Watch later and liked videos, with the YouTube
  Music sign-in; there's a search, Trending, and a Random button that
  searches for a random Wikipedia article. Videos play in YouTube's
  player, keep playing small while you browse (the dock button on the
  player bar sends a video there and goes back to the page you were on),
  fill the window with Full App, and the player bar controls them with
  the same buttons as the music.
- The sidebar and the side panel can be dragged to any width.
